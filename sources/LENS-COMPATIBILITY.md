## Coverage and adapter notes

- **Sony E:** All six Sony lenses are full-frame FE lenses. They mount natively on the four full-frame Sony bodies. On the APS-C Sony ZV-E10 II they mount natively but the camera uses an APS-C crop, so the effective field of view is about 1.5 times the marked focal length.
- **Canon RF:** The R5, R6 Mark II and R8 are full-frame RF bodies. All six Canon lenses are full-frame RF lenses. No EF lens or EF-to-RF adapter is used.
- **Nikon Z:** The Z8 and Zf are full-frame Z bodies. All six NIKKOR Z lenses are full-frame Z lenses. No F-mount lens or FTZ adapter is used.
- **Fujifilm X:** The X-T5 and X-S20 are APS-C X bodies. All six Fujinon XF lenses are APS-C X lenses. The X100VI cannot use them because its lens is fixed.
- **Leica L:** The Panasonic Lumix S5 IIX is a full-frame L-mount body. The six lenses use the native L mount; the Sigma lenses are L-mount versions, not E-mount or EF-mount versions.
- **Not represented:** The live catalog has no Canon EF, Nikon F, Micro Four Thirds or Fujifilm G camera body. No lenses were added for those mounts, and no adapter is listed as a substitute for a native lens.
