## 1. Introduction and attribution policy

All published catalog photographs were obtained from Wikimedia Commons and carry CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA licences. Every published photograph has an exact-model check, a source page, a creator, a licence, a licence URL and attribution text. No AI-generated image, generic substitute or similar-model substitution is used. B&H Photo Video, BIG Camera, ZoomCamera and manufacturer pages were used as identification references only. Images without a confirmed reuse licence were not published.

Every published photograph was re-rendered for the refresh on a **1200 × 1200** square canvas. The product is scaled without upscaling to fit a **1080 × 1080** content box, centred, and the canvas is filled with a neutral colour sampled from the photograph edge so the original studio background is extended rather than replaced. Products are not stretched, retouched or altered. The exact processing string is stored per image in the JSON manifest.
